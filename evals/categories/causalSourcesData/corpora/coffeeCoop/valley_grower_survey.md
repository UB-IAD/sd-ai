# Survey of farms outside the cooperative, 2025

We spoke to 85 non-member farms in the valley about whether they would sign on with the cooperative.

- Most named the price we pay growers as the main reason they would sign on, and several said a neighbour's better price had changed their mind. In the seasons when the price paid to growers rose, the number of farms signing on the following season rose with it.
- Only about 120 farms in the valley are still outside the cooperative, and many of them are very small or sell to relatives. The field office expects fewer farms to sign on each year as the number of outside farms shrinks: with fewer farms left outside the cooperative, there are simply fewer that could sign on.
