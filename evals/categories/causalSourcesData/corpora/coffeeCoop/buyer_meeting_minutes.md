# Minutes: meeting with roasting buyers, September 2025

Present: cooperative manager, export officer, three buyers from two roasting companies.

- The buyers repeated that they will only offer a forward contract when we can guarantee the volume of coffee behind it. When our volume of coffee went up last year, two of them offered forward contracts for the first time. A bigger volume of coffee means more forward contracts on offer.
- Forward contracts fix a price well above the street price at harvest. The export officer noted that the price we pay growers per bag is higher in years when more of the crop goes out under forward contracts, because the contract premium is passed straight on to growers.
- Action: export officer to draft quality specifications for the 2026 contracts.
