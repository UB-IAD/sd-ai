# Harvest summary, 2025 season

Prepared by the cooperative's field office.

- This season 214 member farms delivered cherry to the two washing stations, up from 160 two seasons ago.
- Yields per hectare were flat across the three seasons, so the rise in the volume of coffee we sent for export came almost entirely from having more member farms delivering. Each extra member farm adds roughly 18 bags of coffee in a normal year.
- Rainfall in March was below average at the upper stations; drying took longer but cup scores held.
