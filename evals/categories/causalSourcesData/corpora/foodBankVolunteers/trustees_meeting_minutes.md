# Minutes: trustees' meeting, 22 September 2025

- The number of households we serve has risen from 310 to 470 a week since last winter, mainly because of rent rises in the area.
- The coordinator reported that serving more households has pushed up hours per volunteer: each extra distribution slot needs packing, driving and front-desk cover. More households served means more hours for each volunteer.
- Agreed to approach two supermarkets about regular surplus collections.
