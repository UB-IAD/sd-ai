# Rota coordinator's notes, autumn 2025

- The Tuesday and Friday distributions each need about 120 hours of help to run.
- Hours per volunteer depend directly on how many of us there are. When the number of volunteers on the rota is higher, the same work is shared out and hours per volunteer go down; in weeks when the number of volunteers is low, everyone on the rota does more hours.
- The new van booking sheet is working well.
