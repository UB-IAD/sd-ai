# Exit survey, 2025

We asked the 27 volunteers who stepped down this year why they went.

- Nineteen named burnout as their main reason. In our wellbeing check-ins, burnout rises with hours per volunteer: those doing more than eight hours a week were three times as likely to say they felt burnt out as those doing three or four.
- Burnout is the main reason volunteers leave. Of those who said they felt burnt out at the spring check-in, more than half had stepped down by the autumn, against one in ten of the rest.
