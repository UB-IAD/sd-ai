# Operations review, first half of 2025

- At the end of June the backlog stood at 64 jobs, against 38 a year earlier.
- The waiting time between a customer approving a design and the fit starting has grown in step with the backlog. When the backlog is longer, customers wait longer, because each crew can only start the next job once the one before is finished. The average waiting time is now eleven weeks.
- Adding a fitter cuts the waiting time. In March, when two fitters came back from long-term leave, the waiting time fell by nearly three weeks within a month; more fitters means a shorter waiting time for every customer in the queue.
- Offcut waste was 6% of worktop material, in line with last year.
