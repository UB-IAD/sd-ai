# Showroom sales notes, summer 2025

Notes from the sales team's monthly debriefs.

- Customers ask about the waiting time at the first meeting. Since the waiting time passed ten weeks, more of them have gone to competitors who can fit sooner. The longer the waiting time we quote, the fewer new orders we take.
- Showroom visits are still where our business comes from: most new orders come from people who came in for a showroom visit, and in months with more showroom visits we take more new orders.
