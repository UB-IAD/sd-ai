# Growers' association survey, 2025

Thirty-three businesses replied.

- Profit has followed the wholesale price closely, since growing costs have hardly changed. When the wholesale price fell in 2024, most respondents reported lower profit, and the few whose prices held kept their margins. A higher wholesale price means higher profit.
- The number of new growers starting each year rose in the years when profit was good and dropped off sharply once profit fell. Several people who came to our training days said they had shelved plans to start because the sums no longer worked; when profit is good, more new growers start.
