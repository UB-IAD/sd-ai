# Regional produce market report: specialty mushrooms, 2025

- There are now 41 specialty mushroom growers in the region selling through the two wholesale markets, compared with 23 in 2021.
- Most run one or two grow rooms and harvest all year. As the number of growers in the region has risen, the supply of mushrooms reaching the markets has risen with it; each grower sends roughly 90 kilos a week. More growers means more mushroom supply.
- Oyster and lion's mane make up four-fifths of what is sold.
