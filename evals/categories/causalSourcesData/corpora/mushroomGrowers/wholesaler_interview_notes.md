# Interview notes: two produce wholesalers, October 2025

- Both wholesalers said the wholesale price for oyster mushrooms has fallen by about a third since 2022, and both put it down to supply. When the supply of mushrooms is heavy, buyers can pick and choose and the wholesale price drops; in weeks when the supply of mushrooms is light, the price firms up.
- Restaurant buying holds the price up. One wholesaler said that when restaurant orders rise, as they do before the holidays, the wholesale price rises with them; restaurant demand is the main reason prices have not fallen further.
