# Occupational health review, 2025

- Sickness absence among frontline staff averaged 7.9% this year, the highest since records began.
- Staff who worked the most overtime were the most likely to be off sick. Of the 60 paramedics with the most overtime hours, 41 had at least one spell off sick with stress, exhaustion or a back injury; among the 60 with the least overtime it was 12. Our view is that sustained overtime is driving sickness absence up.
- Recommended: a cap of 12 overtime hours a week per person.
