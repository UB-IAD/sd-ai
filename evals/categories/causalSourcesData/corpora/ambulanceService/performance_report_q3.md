# Performance report, third quarter 2025

- The average response time to the most urgent category of call was 9 minutes 40 seconds, against a target of 8 minutes.
- Response times were shortest on the days with the most crews on shift. On the 14 days when we had 30 or more crews on shift during the day, the average response time was under 8 minutes; with fewer crews on the road, the nearest free ambulance is further away and response times lengthen.
- Hospital handover delays added to response times. An ambulance waiting outside a hospital to hand over its patient cannot take the next job, and on days when handover delays at the main hospital passed an hour, response times across the county rose by about two minutes.
