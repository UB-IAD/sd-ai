# Data note: parcels per van at the Riverside depot

From the depot's daily scanning records, January to September 2025.

- Parcel volume grew 22% over the period as two online retailers moved their contracts to us. With the same fleet, a higher parcel volume means more parcels per van: the average round went from 118 to 141 parcels per van.
- On days with more vans on the road, the load is spread more thinly and parcels per van falls. On the worst days in August, with only 31 vans in service, rounds averaged 168 parcels per van.
