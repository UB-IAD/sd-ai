# Fleet reliability review, 2025

Prepared by the fleet engineer.

- We recorded 74 breakdowns this year, up from 39 in 2024. Brake, clutch and tyre faults made up most of them.
- Vans whose routine servicing was postponed at least once had more than twice as many breakdowns as vans serviced on schedule. Faults that a service would have caught early are turning into breakdowns on the round: the more servicing is put off, the more breakdowns we see.
- Recovery costs rose by 40%.
