# Member survey, spring 2025

We surveyed 1,200 members of the city bike-share scheme.

- 62% of the members surveyed said a friend, family member or colleague who already rides talked them into it.
- Members say they mention the scheme to people they know regularly; the more members there are, the more often people hear about the scheme from someone they know.
- Of the people who said they heard about the scheme from someone they know, most signed up within two months — hearing about it from a rider is the most common reason people give for signing up.
