# Marketing review, 2025

The scheme has run the same modest poster and radio campaign since launch.

- Sign-up records show a small, steady stream of people who say they signed up because of an advert, in every month since launch, including the first months when hardly anyone knew a rider.
- When the campaign paused for six weeks in 2024 because of a contract change, advert-driven sign-ups fell to almost nothing and recovered when it restarted. More advertising brings more sign-ups.
