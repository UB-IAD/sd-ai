# City planning note: the bike-share catchment

About 50,000 residents live within easy reach of a docking station and could realistically sign up.

- Every sign-up comes out of that pool: each one leaves one fewer resident in the catchment who has not signed up.
- As the pool of residents who have not signed up shrinks, sign-ups slow down, because there are fewer people left who could sign up at all. Planners expect sign-ups to tail off as the catchment fills.
